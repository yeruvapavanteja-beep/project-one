The Platform

One connected loop, from soil to sale

Most apps stop at "what to grow." AgriSmart Connect stays with the farmer through the whole season — and lets customers reserve produce before it's even ripe.

How It Works

From soil sample to doorstep

01

Test & Recommend

Farmer enters soil, season, and water data. Our scoring engine ranks suitable crops with a plain-language "why."

02

Plant & Track

Farmer logs planting and updates growth stage with photos — visible to customers as a live progress timeline.

03

Pre-Book & Confirm

Customers reserve quantity from an upcoming harvest. Farmers confirm — no overbooking, ever.

04

Harvest & Deliver

Produce is packed and delivered or picked up. Customer receives fresh produce, straight from the source.
